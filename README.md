## What I do
 
**Research.** Causal inference and spatial data on Argentine elections: turnout, vote transitions, local political competition, and the fiscal money behind it. Regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, ecological inference.
 
**Open data infrastructure.** I build and maintain the geospatial and electoral datasets this kind of work depends on and that nobody was publishing. The main one is [`circuitos_electorales_AR`](https://github.com/tartagalensis/circuitos_electorales_AR): the electoral circuit geometries for all of Argentina, harmonized and updated through 2025.
 
**Data applied to a business.** Co-owner of a five-location pharmacy chain in northern Salta. Demand forecasting, inventory, pricing, receivables, and automating whatever is still done by hand. Same toolkit, much shorter feedback loop.

## Working papers
 
**"Who Votes Because They Have To?"** (2026) — Sharp regression discontinuity on the age thresholds of Argentina's compulsory voting rule, mandatory at 18 and voluntary at 70, across ~35.9M individual voter records from the October 2025 election, merged with census deprivation indices through a spatial join. *MSc dissertation, LSE.*
 
**"Los huérfanos de la política: un estudio sobre la fragmentación de las bases populares del peronismo (2013–2023)"** (2024) — How the split between formal and informal workers, and the rise of post-2001 social movements, reshaped Peronism's internal structure and its electoral base. *MA thesis, UTDT. In preparation for submission.*
